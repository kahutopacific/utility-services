Kahuto Pacific provides subsurface utility investigation across Fiji and the Pacific using Ground Penetrating Radar and EM locating technology. Our services are structured into three clear levels, choose the one that matches your project scope and budget.

Service Levels
LEVEL 1
Site Clearance 		Fastest turnaround

Know what's in the ground before you dig. Our crew locates and marks all detectable utilities on your site, and you receive a photo report documenting what was found.

—   Field marks on ground (spray paint & flags)
—   Geotagged site photos
—   SUI Photo Report (PDF)
—   Basic utility schedule

Best for: Contractors, builders & tradies, plumbers & electricians, small works, road openings & reinstatement works, and pre-excavation safety.

LEVEL 2
Site Clearance + GPS Pickups 		 

Everything in Level 1, plus surveyed GPS pickups for every detected utility — delivered as an Excel register in Fiji Map Grid and WGS84 coordinates.

—   Everything in Level 1
—   GPS-located utility pickups (FMG & WGS84)
—   Excel Utility Register (XLSX) with coordinates, utility type, indicative depth
—   Basic AS 5488 quality level assignment

LEVEL 2 OPTIONAL CAD OPTIONS

Build your drawing, layer by layer

Level 2 gives you site marks and an Excel list of where every utility is. If you would also like it as a CAD drawing, start with Option A, then choose one way to add context; surveyed surface features (Option B) or an aerial photo (Option C). Tap each one to see what it adds.

Tapping here doesn't send anything to us. When you've decided, copy your selection using the button at the bottom and paste it into your reply, or just quote the option letters (A, B, C).

Turn the Excel list into a proper drawing. Every pipe and cable we found is drawn as a line, colour-coded by type, with its approximate depth written on it.

  • A drawing file your engineer or draftsperson can open (DWG / DXF)
  • Fiji Map Grid, plus a WGS84 copy for Google Earth
  • No background — just the utilities

Option BAdd what's on the surface

We survey the things you can see — manholes, pits, poles, valve boxes, kerbs and building edges — as points and lines your draftsperson can snap to.

  • Done on the same site visit, so no extra callout
  • Shows how underground services connect to what's above
  • Best for design work and small sites, driveways and compounds

OPTIONAL ADD-ON

Potholing / NDD Verification

Subcontracted hydrovac excavation to physically expose and verify utility location & depth. Achieves QL-A (highest quality level) per AS 5488.

—   Physical exposure of utilities at critical crossings
—   Supervised by Kahuto survey crew
—   Backfill & surface reinstatement included

OPTIONAL ADD-ON

Drone Orthophoto Basemap

We fly the DJI M3E, process a to-scale orthophoto tied to the same survey control as your utility pickups, and draw the utilities over it in CAD — the photo provides the site context, so no separate surface-feature survey is included. Deliverables: GeoTIFF, JPEG, PDF, ECW + CAD drawing with utility overlay (DWG/PDF). Priced by site area — request when enquiring.

Available with Level 2 (with the Option A) and Level 3.
